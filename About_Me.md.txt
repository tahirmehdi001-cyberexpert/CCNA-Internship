## 🚀 About Me

I am a **Computer Science student** with a strong passion for Networking and Cybersecurity, focused on developing practical technical expertise and building a career in the cybersecurity industry.

I have successfully completed my **Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA)** certification and am currently pursuing **CCNP-level networking knowledge** to strengthen my understanding of enterprise networking, routing, switching, and advanced network troubleshooting.

Alongside my academic studies, I am gaining hands-on networking experience at **Corvit Systems, Rawalpindi**, where I work with Cisco routers and switches, practice network configuration, implement routing and switching technologies, and develop practical troubleshooting skills.

I am also continuously expanding my cybersecurity knowledge through technical learning, practical labs, simulations, and security projects, with an interest in ethical hacking, penetration testing, and network defense.

My long-term ambition is to become a skilled cybersecurity professional capable of designing, managing, analyzing, and securing modern IT infrastructures.
